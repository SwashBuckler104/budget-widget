Budget Widget turns every bank, card and UPI SMS into a transaction waiting in an inbox. Sort it in
one tap from the notification, the home-screen Quick-Sort widget or the app, and close the day
before midnight to keep your streak.

* Notifications with your daily categories as buttons
* Quick-Sort widget: the next payment and category chips on your home screen
* Insight widget: today, this month against last month, the last 7 days
* Analysis for any week, month, year or date range: spending and income pie charts, trends and
  top merchants
* Search, filters, editing, cash entries, bulk actions, swipe with undo
* Detects the same payment reported by two banks
* CSV export, JSON backup and restore

Private by construction: the app has no internet permission, so your financial data never leaves
your phone. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

SMS parsing uses PennyWise parser-core by the PennyWise authors (AGPL-3.0), unmodified.
